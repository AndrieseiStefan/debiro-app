# My Companies and Company Switcher (E1-021/022)

`/profile/companies` and `/en/profile/companies` are global-user routes with a **Profil > Companiile mele** breadcrumb. The list and creation form follow [mockup 21](../design/mockups/21-Profile-my-companies.png). The bottom-left authenticated company control opens the viewport-contained switcher from [mockup 22](../design/mockups/22-change-company-popup.png). Both interfaces and the [profile dropdown](profile-dropdown.md) read one browser-memory accessible-company state. English copy awaits product-owner review.

The source starts with two accessible companies: Demo Company SRL (current-user Administrator, Professional plan) and Global Clean Services (current-user Reviewer, Business plan). Each record owns its company metadata, memberships, and subscription fixture; the user's role is resolved through that company's active membership. One company ID is active. Switching from either interface updates the sidebar label, profile role/company, Company Settings, and active indicators without changing the current route. The switcher closes on selection, trigger toggle, outside pointer interaction, or Escape; Escape restores focus to its trigger. Its footer opens My Companies.

The form requires trimmed company name, industry, tax ID/CUI, and country, and rejects a case-insensitive duplicate CUI among accessible companies. The current user is the read-only initial administrator. Successful local creation adds an organization and current-user Administrator membership with a separate Starter trial/subscription fixture, then makes it active in the same state change. The count updates in all three interfaces. Browser refresh resets locally created companies and the active selection; no account, organization, billing, or authentication backend is involved.

The existing Members & Access and Plan & Billing screens read the active company's own membership/subscription data. Companies without a payment method or invoices show empty billing states, never Demo Company billing data. Other existing authenticated screens are still backed only by Demo Company fixtures: when another company is active, those routes remain accessible but display a localized empty demo state rather than another company's suppliers, documents, requirements, dashboard figures, or notifications. No cross-company records are copied. No screenshot baseline is approved or committed.
