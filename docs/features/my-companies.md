# My Companies and Company Switcher (E1-021/022)

`/profile/companies` and `/en/profile/companies` are global-user routes with a **Profil > Companiile mele** breadcrumb. The list and creation form follow [mockup 21](../design/mockups/21-Profile-my-companies.png). The bottom-left authenticated company control opens the viewport-contained switcher from [mockup 22](../design/mockups/22-change-company-popup.png). Both interfaces and the [profile dropdown](profile-dropdown.md) read one browser-memory accessible-company state. English copy awaits product-owner review.

The source starts with two accessible companies: Demo Company SRL (current-user Administrator, Professional plan) and Global Clean Services (current-user Reviewer, Business plan). Each record owns its company metadata, memberships, and subscription fixture; the user's role is resolved through that company's active membership. One company ID is active. E1-031B switching from either interface activates the selected accessible company and enters its locale-matched Dashboard (`/dashboard` or `/en/dashboard`), rather than retaining the previous route. The switcher closes on selection, trigger toggle, outside pointer interaction, or Escape; Escape restores focus to its trigger. Its footer opens My Companies.

The form requires trimmed company name, industry, tax ID/CUI, and country, and rejects a case-insensitive duplicate CUI among accessible companies. The current user is the read-only initial administrator. Successful local creation adds an organization and current-user Administrator membership with a separate Starter trial/subscription fixture, then makes it active in the same state change and enters its Dashboard. Returning to My Companies shows the retained record and updated accessible-company count. Browser refresh resets locally created companies and the active selection; no account, organization, billing, or authentication backend is involved.

Company identity now uses shared industry/country IDs and feeds the editable [Company Profile](company-profile.md). Created companies show their creation values there, with unknown contact/address fields empty. Company state survives SPA module reinitialization and locale navigation within the same tab, not refresh or other tabs.

E1-032A adds accepted [received invitations](my-profile-invitations.md) to this same accessible-company list without activating them. Existing membership/access is reused without duplication. Global user edits update the read-only initial administrator and live current-member labels; company-owned profile/subscription data stays independent. My Companies layout and existing creation/switch behavior are unchanged.

Members & Access and Plan & Billing read the active company's own membership/subscription data and live Company identity. Companies without a payment method or invoices show empty billing states, never Demo Company billing data. E1-031 exposes complete company-owned operational workspaces with honest first-run states and an existing Add Vendor entry; see [Company workspaces](company-workspaces.md). No cross-company records are copied. No screenshot baseline is approved or committed.
