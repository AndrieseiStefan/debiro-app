# My Companies and Company Switcher (E1-021/022)

`/profile/companies` and `/en/profile/companies` are global-user routes with a **Profil > Companiile mele / Profile > My Companies** breadcrumb. E1-032A1 uses the shared breadcrumb component with a locale-preserving Profile parent link to `/profile` or `/en/profile`. The list and creation form follow [mockup 21](../design/mockups/21-Profile-my-companies.png). The bottom-left authenticated company control opens the viewport-contained switcher from [mockup 22](../design/mockups/22-change-company-popup.png). Both interfaces and the [profile dropdown](profile-dropdown.md) read one browser-memory accessible-company state. English copy awaits product-owner review.

The source starts with two accessible companies: Demo Company SRL (current-user Administrator, Professional plan) and Global Clean Services (current-user Reviewer, Business plan). Each record owns its company metadata, memberships, and subscription fixture; the user's role is resolved through that company's active membership. One company ID is active. E1-032A1 explicit switching from a global Profile route activates the selected company without navigation or page remount: the active card/context updates and form state remains. Switching from a company-scoped route still enters its locale-matched Dashboard under E1-031B. The switcher closes on selection, trigger toggle, outside pointer interaction, or Escape; Escape restores focus to its trigger. Its footer opens My Companies.

E1-032A3 keeps the existing 260px switcher anchored directly to the company button: desktop prefers top-start with an 8px gap and matching left edge. Its height is measured at the final panel width and includes borders; resize/scroll and panel-size changes recalculate placement. If it cannot fit above, it falls back beside the trigger on the right and shifts/constrains vertically inside 12px viewport gutters, with existing overflow scrolling. Compact/mobile layouts retain their existing below-header, trigger-right-aligned placement and viewport containment. Content, styling and switching/navigation contracts are unchanged.

The form requires trimmed company name, industry, tax ID/CUI, and country, and rejects a case-insensitive duplicate CUI among accessible companies. The current user is the read-only initial administrator. Successful local creation adds an organization and current-user Administrator membership with a separate Starter trial/subscription fixture, then makes it active in the same state change and enters its Dashboard. Returning to My Companies shows the retained record and updated accessible-company count. Browser refresh resets locally created companies and the active selection; no account, organization, billing, or authentication backend is involved.

Company identity now uses shared industry/country IDs and feeds the editable [Company Profile](company-profile.md). Created companies show their creation values there, with unknown contact/address fields empty. Company state survives SPA module reinitialization and locale navigation within the same tab, not refresh or other tabs.

E1-032A adds accepted [received invitations](my-profile-invitations.md) to this same accessible-company list without activating them. Existing membership/access is reused without duplication. Global user edits update the read-only initial administrator and live current-member labels; company-owned profile/subscription data stays independent. My Companies layout and existing creation/switch behavior are unchanged.

Members & Access and Plan & Billing read the active company's own membership/subscription data and live Company identity. E1-032Bd confirmed local upgrades propagate to these existing plan badges through the shared subscription and centralized demo catalog; trial state is not a tier. Companies without a payment method or invoices show empty billing states, never Demo Company billing data. E1-031 exposes complete company-owned operational workspaces with honest first-run states and an existing Add Vendor entry; see [Company workspaces](company-workspaces.md). No cross-company records are copied. No screenshot baseline is approved or committed.
