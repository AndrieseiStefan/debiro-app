# Document Requirements (E1-006, E1-023)

`/requirements` and `/en/requirements` use [canonical mockup 06](../design/mockups/06-document-requirements.png) for the authenticated template workspace and [23A](../design/mockups/23A-Add-suggested-document-to-template.png) / [23B](../design/mockups/23B-Add-personalized-document-to-template.png) for the Add Document drawer. The top-level breadcrumb is **Dashboard > Cerințe**. The visible brand is plain uppercase `DEBIRO`.

The page reuses the authenticated shell, page header, breadcrumbs, shared Drawer, Button, and Surface. Existing Demo Company templates begin from deterministic fixtures; template search, selection, required/optional rules, expiry alerts, and validity controls are browser-local. Cancel resets unsaved edits to existing rules, and Save applies them to browser memory. Settings and preview tabs, template duplication/deletion, and row menus remain inactive.

`Șablon nou` and `Adaugă șablon nou` switch the existing right workspace into a new-template draft. A new template requires a unique normalized name within the active company, a category from the shared vendor category list, and at least one document. The draft starts with an explicit zero-document state. Saving creates a company-scoped browser-memory template and selects it; canceling discards it. A dirty draft asks for confirmation before cancellation, template selection, sidebar navigation, or workspace switching. A direct page reload is protected by the browser's standard unsaved-changes prompt; browser-memory data does not persist across reloads.

The Add Document drawer supports local catalog search and a custom-document mode. Catalog entries have stable IDs and read-only localized names; a template document stores its catalog ID **or** its custom name, never both. Required status, expiry warning, and validity belong to each template document, not to the shared catalog. Custom names are trimmed and checked against template duplicates with case-, whitespace-, and accent-insensitive normalization; descriptions are limited to 200 characters. The simulated global catalog-candidate list records custom documents only after a new template is saved, never when its draft is abandoned. It is not a published catalog or a service integration.

Templates and documents are isolated by the active browser-memory company. Catalog suggestions may be shared, but company templates are not. Romanian and English interfaces use the same catalog identities and rules. At reduced widths the template list stacks above the editor, the rules table scrolls within its focusable region, and the Drawer stays viewport-contained with internal scrolling. The mockups remain unchanged and generated visual captures are ignored review artifacts, not approved baselines.

There is no backend, API, database, authentication, authorization, assignment, document upload, processing, notification delivery, or durable persistence in this feature.
