# Company Plan & Billing (E1-019)

`/company/settings/billing` and `/en/company/settings/billing` render the active company's Plan & Billing page in the authenticated shell. The page follows [canonical mockup 19](../design/mockups/19-company-settings-plans-pay.png) and uses the Dashboard > Company Settings > Plan & Billing breadcrumb. English copy awaits product-owner review.

Each [accessible company](my-companies.md) owns its subscription/trial fixture, seat limit, billing profile, plan capabilities, and invoice history; these are not properties of the global user. The default Demo Company fixture also has a masked payment-method display and sample invoices. The current user's company role is read from that company's membership. The page reads the same company-keyed browser-local invitation state as [Members & Access](company-members-access.md). Active members count toward seats, pending invitations do not, and available seats are derived from the active company's seat limit minus active members. Accessible-company count remains a user-context display only; subscriptions remain separate by company.

Prices, payment date, payment method, and invoices are deterministic sample data where present. A newly created company has its own Starter trial, no payment method, and no invoices; corresponding empty states do not reuse Demo Company values. Legal name/CUI read the canonical Company, including [Company Profile](company-profile.md) updates. Billing-specific email/address/contact and subscription/payment/invoice records remain independent and unchanged. Upgrade, billing-profile update, card change, invoice download, invoice-list, and invoice-row actions remain visibly disabled. No provider, checkout, real card entry/storage, invoice resource, subscription mutation, entitlement enforcement, or billing authorization is implemented. Invoice history scrolls within its panel at reduced widths. Generated screenshots are ignored; no visual baseline has been approved or committed.
