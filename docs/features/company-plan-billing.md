# Company Plan & Billing (E1-019)

`/company/settings/billing` and `/en/company/settings/billing` render the active company's Plan & Billing page in the authenticated shell. The page follows [canonical mockup 19](../design/mockups/19-company-settings-plans-pay.png) and uses the Dashboard > Company Settings > Plan & Billing breadcrumb. English copy awaits product-owner review.

The existing E1-018 company fixture owns the subscription, seat limit, trial, billing profile, masked payment-method display, plan capabilities, and invoice history; these are not properties of the global user. The current user's company role is read from that company's membership. The page reads the same browser-local invitation state as [Members & Access](company-members-access.md). Active members count toward seats, pending invitations do not, and available seats are derived from the company seat limit minus active members. Accessible-company count remains a user-context display only; subscriptions remain separate by company.

Prices, payment date, payment method, and invoices are deterministic sample data. Upgrade, billing-profile update, card change, invoice download, invoice-list, and invoice-row actions are visibly disabled demo controls. No provider, checkout, real card entry/storage, invoice resource, subscription mutation, entitlement enforcement, or billing authorization is implemented. Company switching and Company Profile are still unimplemented. Invoice history scrolls within its own panel at reduced widths. Generated screenshots are ignored; no visual baseline has been approved or committed.
