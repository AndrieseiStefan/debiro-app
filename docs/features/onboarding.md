# Onboarding Screen

E1-002 implements the first-step screen in [canonical mockup 02](../design/mockups/02-onboarding.png) at `/onboarding` and `/en/onboarding`; E1-012 extends that same shell with [Step 2](../design/mockups/12-onboarding-step2.png) and [Step 3](../design/mockups/13-onboarding-step3.png). The Romanian mockup copy is preserved with placeholder branding replaced by `DEBIRO`; English is translated for review. The page reuses PublicHeader, Button, Field, Surface, and Divider. A deterministic typed fixture supplies visible sample values; editing, password visibility, native required-field/password-rule validation, and terms selection are local to the browser.

Step 1 is mandatory and its valid Continue action opens Step 2. Step 2 offers a local construction or general template choice and an editable initial document checklist; Continue and Configure later both open Step 3. Step 3 starts with two fixture suppliers and supports adding or removing rows. Populated rows need a name and valid contact email to finish; fully empty rows do not block completion, and Skip goes directly to Dashboard. Completed Step 2 can be revisited from Step 3 without losing its local selections. All three steps stay on the same route; refresh resets the fixture flow. No requirements are persisted, no invitation is sent, and no account, authentication, backend, or API is created. The industry, company-size, and country selectors reproduce the visible Step 1 choices only, without inventing unapproved option sets. The shared top header omits the former secondary brand tagline and contains only `DEBIRO`, help, and RO/EN; help moves to a centered second row when those elements no longer fit. Help and legal text have no destinations yet.

The desktop mountain landscape is a generated decorative approximation; the right quote illustrations are built in CSS, including simplified construction and supplier motifs for Steps 2 and 3 because exact source artwork is unavailable. Neither the mockup nor a captured screenshot is rendered as the interface. Responsive reflow follows the [UI foundation](../design/ui-foundation.md#responsive-contract). Generated visual captures remain unapproved and ignored.
