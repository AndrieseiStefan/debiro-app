## Presentation and navigation (E1-032Bh)

Hero, benefits and trust form three normal-flow flex groups with `min-height: calc(100svh - var(--public-header-height))`, accounting for the 66px desktop header. The updated E1-032Bh3 product decision distributes surplus viewport height **between the three groups**, using `justify-content: space-between` and the existing minimum gaps. Both internal gaps grow equally with available free height and stop shrinking at their content-safe minima. Trust is bottom-aligned in the presentation's content area: only `--space-10` (40px) separates its lowest logo/statistics content from the section boundary, with no additional dynamic whitespace after it. Short-height content can exceed the viewport and scroll naturally, without fixed sub-block heights, clipping or compression. Pricing follows the entire presentation and never enters the initial viewport. Hero typography, blue accents, CSS clouds and live HTML/CSS preview are retained; the teaser's mountains/handwriting no longer sit beside a teaser CTA. Feature cards reflow 3/2/1, and below 1200px hero copy precedes its contained proportional preview. Inter remains provisional; no canonical mobile mockup exists.

Desktop Hero content starts 32px below the header. From 768px upward, the duplicate Hero bottom padding is removed, the minimum flex gap is 20px and the trust row retains a further 12px minimum top margin. The former 36–48px desktop heading-gap target is not an upper bound: taller viewports intentionally enlarge the two internal gaps, never the bottom padding. Tablet keeps the stacked composition; mobile below 768px retains its existing padding, minimum gaps and reflows. The existing compact-height content sizing (≤1076px high, ≥1200px wide) still uses the available benefits-introduction width, existing card padding and a proportional preview capped at 640px; no fonts, controls, cards or preview implementation are changed. At 1448×900 the natural presentation still ends around y=932 with 40px after Trust, scrolling instead of squeezing content. At 1920×1080 the presentation fills the viewport and Trust ends at y=1040, exactly 40px before Pricing.

The existing dropdown-looking controls had no menus or child destinations. They retain their chevrons but are now ordinary keyboard-accessible native section links: `#product` (hero), `#solutions` (benefits), `#pricing` (full catalog), `#resources`. No fake dropdown is added. The header remains non-sticky, so targets cannot be obscured beneath a fixed overlay; no scroll offsets or scroll engine are required. Native anchors preserve browser Back/Forward and direct access. E1-032Bh3 enables native root smooth scrolling only while Landing is rendered and motion is allowed; reduced motion retains immediate navigation. The rule does not affect Login, Onboarding or authenticated pages and adds no scroll engine or navigation handler. Locale activation reads the current browser hash and passes it to the existing locale router, without a separate anchor state store. Below the existing 864px header collision point, marketing links occupy their own row rather than disappearing. Existing account-action and pre-auth help-only reflows are preserved.

Resources is an explicitly approved compact coming-soon target with only a heading and the supplied RO/EN preparation message. It contains no fake guides, documentation, tutorials, Login/Onboarding CTAs or new route. Actual resource content remains open for product review.

## Shared pricing

They share rank ordering, tier names, prices, capability-derived bullets, icons, typography, Surface/Button treatment and 3/2/1 grid. No duplicate public card CSS or pricing array exists. The component reads only explicit presentation props/catalog/translations, never company/session/private billing state.

Marketing displays available tiers, with provisional centralized `marketingPlanConfig.featuredPlanId = Professional` as the sole blue emphasis; it does not infer popularity from company state or substitute another tier if unavailable. It never displays current-plan or trial metadata. Every public tier action uses locale-aware Onboarding, without persisting a selected tier, changing subscriptions or processing payments. The redundant View All Plans action is removed. Every card retains explicit E1 demo-price labeling. An empty displayable catalog yields a compact truthful unavailable state. Upgrade retains its separate current/next-tier/trial states and confirmed company-scoped actions; see [billing](company-plan-billing.md).

### Dark Premium presentation (E1-032Bh1)

The approved task brief supersedes the former light Pricing presentation with a full-width DEBIRO navy gradient and light shared cards. The configured featured card gets restrained blue glow/border/ribbon emphasis. Two pointer-transparent, accessibility-hidden decorative pseudo-layers animate transform/opacity only over 16s/20s alternate cycles. Their clipping belongs solely to the background layer, not the content/cards. Mobile omits the secondary glow and reduces the primary intensity. Reduced motion disables ambient animation and card translation/transitions while keeping static emphasis and existing focus/hover control states. No library, image, video, canvas, scroll loop or subscription dependency is added. Upgrade has no matching marketing selectors and preserves its existing surface, dimensions, shadows, current/next-tier/trial state and confirmation/ownership behavior.
