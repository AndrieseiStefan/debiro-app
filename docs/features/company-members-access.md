# Company Members & Access (E1-018)

`/company/settings/members` and `/en/company/settings/members` are Company Settings routes. The expandable final sidebar section contains Company Profile, Members & Access, and Plan & Billing; the latter two navigate, while Company Profile remains unimplemented. The page belongs to the active `Demo Company SRL` fixture and follows [canonical mockup 18](../design/mockups/18-company-settings-access-members.png), with the authenticated Dashboard > Company Settings > Members & Access breadcrumb. English copy awaits product-owner review.

Membership roles are scoped to `companyId`, not stored as global user roles. Administrator has full operational and company-setting access; Reviewer can review and confirm documents and may manage operational compliance data under later authorization rules; Viewer is read-only. Active fixture memberships can reference a user; a pending invitation has no authenticated user or session. The current plan, seat limit, trial state, and accessible-company count are display-only fixture data associated with the company context. The active-member count excludes pending invitations; the page shows pending invitations separately.

Invite Member reuses the shared Drawer. Name, email, and role are required; the current company is read-only. Email uniqueness is checked case-insensitively only within that company's existing members and browser-local invitations. A valid submission adds a pending company membership row labeled `Invitație trimisă`, retained through normal client navigation but cleared by refresh. This action does not send email, create a Clerk identity, accept an invitation, provision permissions, or change subscription/billing. [Plan & Billing](company-plan-billing.md) reads the same company memberships and seat limit. Company Profile, company switching, and member edit/delete actions are not implemented. The table scrolls within its panel on reduced widths, and the role explanation and summary cards reflow without page-level horizontal overflow. No screenshot baseline has been approved or committed.
