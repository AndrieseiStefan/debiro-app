# Company Members & Access (E1-018)

`/company/settings/members` and `/en/company/settings/members` are Company Settings routes. The expandable final sidebar section contains Company Profile, Members & Access, and Plan & Billing; the latter two navigate, while Company Profile remains unimplemented. The page follows [canonical mockup 18](../design/mockups/18-company-settings-access-members.png) for the default Demo Company fixture, with the authenticated Dashboard > Company Settings > Members & Access breadcrumb. It now reads the [active company's](my-companies.md) own memberships, plan, and trial after local switching. English copy awaits product-owner review.

Membership roles are scoped to `companyId`, not stored as global user roles. Administrator has full operational and company-setting access; Reviewer can review and confirm documents and may manage operational compliance data under later authorization rules; Viewer is read-only. Active fixture memberships can reference a user; a pending invitation has no authenticated user or session. Plan, seat limit, and trial state belong to the active company; accessible-company count derives from the shared global-user company list. The active-member count excludes pending invitations; the page shows pending invitations separately.

Implemented membership-role visuals share one presentation contract across Members & Access, its Invite Member drawer, My Companies, the company switcher, and the profile dropdown: Administrator is Crown/blue, Reviewer is Shield/violet, and Viewer is Eye/blue-neutral. The active-company role summary changes icon and color with the current membership; role colors are not inferred from user avatars.

Invite Member reuses the shared Drawer. Name, email, and role are required; the current company is read-only. Email uniqueness is checked case-insensitively only within that company's existing members and browser-local invitations. A valid submission adds a pending company membership row labeled `Invitație trimisă`, retained through normal client navigation but cleared by refresh. Invitations are keyed by company and do not appear under another active workspace. This action does not send email, create a Clerk identity, accept an invitation, provision permissions, or change subscription/billing. [Plan & Billing](company-plan-billing.md) reads the same company memberships and seat limit. Company Profile and member edit/delete actions are not implemented. The table scrolls within its panel on reduced widths, and the role explanation and summary cards reflow without page-level horizontal overflow. No screenshot baseline has been approved or committed.
