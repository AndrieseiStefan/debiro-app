# Company Members & Access (E1-018)

`/company/settings/members` and `/en/company/settings/members` are Company Settings routes. The expandable final sidebar section contains working Company Profile, Members & Access and Plan & Billing links. The page follows [canonical mockup 18](../design/mockups/18-company-settings-access-members.png) for the default Demo Company fixture, with the authenticated Dashboard > Company Settings > Members & Access breadcrumb. It reads the [active company's](my-companies.md) own memberships, plan, trial and saved profile identity after local editing/switching. English copy awaits product-owner review.

Membership roles are scoped to `companyId`, not stored as global user roles. Administrator has full operational and company-setting access; Reviewer can review and confirm documents and may manage operational compliance data under later authorization rules; Viewer is read-only. Active fixture memberships can reference a user; a pending invitation has no authenticated user or session. Plan, seat limit, and trial state belong to the active company; accessible-company count derives from the shared global-user company list. The active-member count excludes pending invitations; the page shows pending invitations separately.

Implemented membership-role visuals share one presentation contract across Members & Access, its Invite Member drawer, My Companies, the company switcher, and the profile dropdown: Administrator is Crown/blue, Reviewer is Shield/violet, and Viewer is Eye/blue-neutral. The active-company role summary changes icon and color with the current membership; role colors are not inferred from user avatars.

Invite Member reuses the shared Drawer and RoleSelector (extracted unchanged from its existing radio control). Name, email, and role are required; the current company is read-only. Email uniqueness is checked case-insensitively within active memberships and unexpired pending invitations for that company. E1-032Ba stores outgoing invitations in the same CompanyInvitation/browserSession lifecycle as [My Invitations](my-profile-invitations.md), with recipient name/email, proposed role, inviter, creation/expiry and resolution history. The Members table projects pending records as `Invitație trimisă`, not provisioned memberships. The existing Mihai fixture projects from that source once. New local invitations expire seven days after the deterministic E1 reference; no email, Clerk identity, session or backend is created.

Only the current active-company Administrator can manage another member. The shared RowActionMenu exposes Change role/Remove member for active rows and Change role/Cancel invitation for pending rows. Current-user rows and Reviewer/Viewer views expose no management menu; Invite Member is Administrator-only. The shared role drawer, RoleSelector, Button and ConfirmationDialog provide member/email/current/proposed-role clarity. Role changes require a primary confirmation; removal/cancellation use destructive confirmation, with focus returning to the row or surviving Invite Member button. Local inline status/alert feedback follows the existing pattern, without fake loading/toasts.

Transactions revalidate activeCompanyId, current permission, target ownership/existence, valid role and pending/unexpired state. Active memberships always retain at least one Administrator; pending Administrator invitations do not satisfy that invariant. Unsupported self-management is also rejected in domain logic. Role/removal updates only that company's membership; cancellation retains a cancelled invitation with resolution time, excludes it from pending counts and prevents acceptance. Re-invitation after cancellation creates a new record without erasing history. Later acceptance uses the latest proposed role via the existing membership/access source; role changes alone grant no access. Counts, seat usage and role projections update synchronously. Successful management mutations record one event through existing company audit; failed/cancelled actions record none.

State survives client/module/locale navigation in the same tab; reload/new tabs reset the local demo. [Plan & Billing](company-plan-billing.md) reads the same active memberships and pending projections; pending records do not consume seats. E1-032Bd local plan changes update the existing badge and seat limit from the company subscription/catalog while preserving trial status and days. Canonical Company identity/contact/address are shared with Profile and Billing; only billing email is finance-specific. The table scrolls within its panel on reduced widths, and existing layout/role visuals are unchanged. No screenshot baseline has been approved or committed.
